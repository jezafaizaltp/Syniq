What SYNiq is

Like a teacher who knows you well

Every student has their own path. SYNiq remembers the mistakes you tend to make and the explanations that work for you, then changes how it teaches. It never turns you into a score or a label.

Notices how you learn

It spots patterns in your answers, like mixing up two ideas, and teaches to that.

Explains it another way

Pictures, small steps, a real-life story, or a smaller question. You can switch any time.

Makes mistakes safe

No red crosses, no shame. A mistake is just a clue about what to look at next.

Builds confidence

It guides you to the answer yourself, so you know you can do it again.

How it teaches

Not a spoon-feeding bot. A real teacher.

SYNiq doesn't just hand over answers. It asks the next small question, waits for you to think, and gives a bigger hint only when you need one.

Tell it what you want to learn

Any topic: angle of elevation, photosynthesis, fractions, a poem, the French Revolution.

It finds where you are

One or two friendly questions to see what you already know, so it starts at the right place.

You learn your way

Pictures first, tiny steps, real-life examples, or "let me try first". Meaning and use, not just rules.

Practise, slip, fix it yourself

When an answer goes wrong, it shows you where, and helps you get it right on your own.

Mistakes show the way

See where it went wrong, then make it right

When you share an answer, SYNiq draws your way next to the right path, so the difference is easy to see.

  • It starts with what you got right.
  • It points to the one step that went off course, without blame.
  • It asks you to fix it, with a hint if you want one.
  • Then a similar question, so the new idea sticks.

For every kind of learner

Made for learners whose path is different

Some of us read slowly, some think in pictures, some need things one step at a time, and some use a screen reader. SYNiq changes how it teaches to fit you. No diagnosis needed, no labels, ever.

  • Pictures first. Diagrams, bars and number lines instead of walls of text.
  • Small steps. One idea at a time, with a check before moving on.
  • Easy reading. Short sentences, plain words, a reading-friendly font.
  • Listen and speak. Replies can be read aloud, and you can talk instead of type.
  • Screen reader friendly. Every picture comes with a description in words.
  • No timers. Take as long as you need. Ask again as often as you like.
  • Your language. Write in English, Malayalam or Hindi and it answers in kind.
  • Bigger text, high contrast. Change how it looks in one tap.
  • You're in charge. Say "explain it another way" or "I'm stuck" at any time.

About REAXIS

Made in Calicut, for every learner

REAXIS is an education start-up incubated at Providence Women's College, Calicut, Kerala. We started SYNiq because too many students are taught as if they all learn the same way.

We'd love to hear from schools, teachers, parents and specialists who want to help shape SYNiq.

What we believe

  • Every student can learn when it's taught the way they need.
  • Mistakes are part of learning, never something to be ashamed of.
  • Understanding the meaning and use of an idea matters more than memorising it.
  • Technology should support teachers and families, not replace them.
